Iron and Mat privacy

Iron and Mat is a personal training app used by its owner. It is not a commercial service.

What it stores

Your training log, body measurements and settings are stored in your phone's browser. If you turn on GitHub backup, a copy is saved to a private GitHub repository that you own.

WHOOP data

If you connect WHOOP, the app reads only the data you approve (such as recovery, sleep, strain, workouts and body measurements) to adjust and record your training. WHOOP access tokens are held by your own review server and are never published, sold or shared.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Weekly reviews are written by Claude from your private backup.

Removing access

You can disconnect WHOOP at any time from your WHOOP account settings, and delete your data by clearing the app's site data and deleting your private backup repository.
